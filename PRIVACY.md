### Privacy Policy for Nest SDM Homebridge Integration

**Last Updated:** [09/10/2026] 

This Privacy Policy explains how our personal, private smart home integration handles your data. This integration is designed solely for personal use to bridge Google Nest devices into an Apple HomeKit environment using Homebridge. 

### 1. Data Collection and Usage

This integration only accesses the Google Nest Smart Device Management (SDM) API to allow communication between your Nest devices (such as thermostats, cameras, and doorbells) and your local Homebridge server. 

### 2. Data Storage

All authentication tokens, device identifiers, and configuration data are stored strictly on your local Homebridge server. No data is transmitted to, stored by, or processed by any third-party external servers. 

### 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer your personal information or smart home data to any third parties. 

### 4. Third-Party Services

This integration interacts directly with Google Cloud Services and the Nest SDM API. Your use of those services is governed by Google's overall Privacy Policy.
